How grades work. SP+ and Elo spreads are first fitted to the slate's DraftKings lines (both run more extreme than the market), so an edge is where DK breaks from its usual relationship to the model. PLAY: both models disagree with DK by 3+ points on the same team. LEAN: one by 3+, the other by 1+. Totals: SP+ and a per-play (yards/play × pace) projection, each de-biased to DK, must both miss by 3+ the same way (PLAY if either is 5+). Downgrades: line moved 2+ points toward the other side since open; overs where the total fell 5+ on a rain forecast. Spreads of 25+ and option offenses are flagged, not graded.

Data: DraftKings lines via ESPN's public scoreboard; SP+, EPA, success rate, Elo and results via CollegeFootballData.com. Models don't know about injuries or weather — check both, and always confirm the current price in the DraftKings app. This is a research tool, not a pick; Ballwatcher's actual plays are on Today's Card.
